What to include

Please include the app version, device model, OS version, language, the screen or stage where the issue occurred, steps to reproduce, and a screenshot when possible.

Security: Never send your Apple ID or Google password, full card number, authentication code, or complete receipt. For purchase support, redact personal and payment information.

Purchase restoration

  1. Use the same App Store or Google Play account used for the original purchase.
  2. Open LATTICE ZERO and go to Store.
  3. Select Restore Purchases.
  4. If the entitlement is still missing, restart the app and contact support with the product name and approximate purchase date.

Restoring purchases restores eligible store entitlements. It does not restore all locally stored story or equipment progress.

Advertising privacy

Where required, open Settings → Ad Privacy Options to review available Google advertising consent choices. If the entry is not shown, it may not be required or available for the current region and consent state.

Local data deletion

Use Reset Progress in Settings to clear game progression while preserving eligible store entitlements, or uninstall the app to remove its local app data. Purchase history held by Apple or Google must be managed through the applicable store account.
