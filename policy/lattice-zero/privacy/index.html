English

1. Scope and operator

This Privacy Policy explains how PUNGPUNGSTUDI operates LATTICE ZERO and how information may be processed when you use the app. Contact us at pung0805@gmail.com.

2. Local game data

Game progress, settings, language, local entitlements, equipment, story choices, and operator bond progress are stored on your device. LATTICE ZERO does not require an account and does not operate a separate player-account server.

3. Advertising and consent

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on your region, device, consent choices, and Google configuration, Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising data, app interactions, approximate location derived from network information, diagnostics, consent records, and fraud-prevention information to provide, measure, secure, or personalize ads.

Where required, the app requests advertising consent before requesting ads. You can reopen available advertising privacy choices from the app Settings screen. Declining optional consent does not block the core game.

4. Purchases

Apple App Store or Google Play processes payments. The app receives product identifiers, purchase status, transaction time, and verification information needed to grant or restore an entitlement. We do not receive your full payment-card number or store-account password.

5. Data sharing and third parties

Information may be processed by Google Mobile Ads/UMP and the app store used for payment. Their handling is governed by their respective policies and your device or account settings. We do not sell locally stored game progress to data brokers.

6. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you reset progress in the app, clear app storage where supported, or uninstall the app. Store purchase history and advertising data are retained by Apple, Google, or their partners under their own policies. You may contact us for help understanding or exercising available privacy choices.

7. Children

LATTICE ZERO is not designed or marketed as a children's app. The game contains mature science-fiction themes and adult characters. If we learn that information has been processed contrary to applicable child-privacy requirements, we will take appropriate steps with our service providers.

8. Security and international processing

We limit app-side processing to what is needed for gameplay, advertising, consent, purchase restoration, diagnostics, and fraud prevention. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when the app, law, or third-party services change. The effective date above will be updated, and material changes will be disclosed through this page or the app where appropriate.
