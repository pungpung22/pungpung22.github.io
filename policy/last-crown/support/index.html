What to include

Please include your app version, device model, OS version, language, the affected screen or stage, the approximate time of the issue, steps to reproduce it, and a screenshot when possible. For missing currency or progression, include the balance before and after the issue.

Security: Never send your Apple ID or Google password, full payment-card number, authentication code, private sign-in token, or an unredacted complete receipt.

Account and cloud-save help

  1. Confirm that the device has an active internet connection.
  2. Open Settings → Cloud Account and verify the connected Apple or Google account.
  3. Do not uninstall the app while a cloud-save issue is unresolved.
  4. Contact support with the app version, device, OS, last successful play time, and the missing progress or currency amount.

Purchase restoration

  1. Use the same App Store or Google Play account used for the original purchase.
  2. Open Last Crown → Shop → Restore Purchases.
  3. Restart the app after restoration completes.
  4. If an eligible entitlement is still missing, email the product name and approximate purchase date. Redact personal and payment information from screenshots.

Purchase restoration restores eligible store entitlements. It is not a replacement for cloud-save synchronization of all gameplay progress.

Advertising privacy

Where required, open Settings → Ad Privacy Options to review available advertising-consent choices. Rewarded ads are optional; contact support if a completed rewarded ad does not grant its displayed reward.

Account deletion and local data

Signed-in players can request account deletion from Settings → Cloud Account. Local-only progression can be cleared using Reset Progress in Settings or by uninstalling the app. Store purchase history is managed by Apple or Google under the applicable store account.
